# Credits

## The game

Endless Labyrinth is designed and directed by Russell Hinderman (DevHinderman2026).

**AI assisted.** The game's code, including its modes, its sound design code, the event system and the developer tools, was written with the help of AI: Claude, by Anthropic, working under Russell's direction. Russell decides how every part of the game works, sounds and plays, and tests it by ear; the AI writes and checks the code that carries those decisions out.

## Sounds

Every recorded sound in Endless Labyrinth comes from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) and is used under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), except one, a set of announcer lines downloaded for the web version and not used, which comes from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The Pixabay license allows free use, including in games, and allows editing. It does not require credit, but every recording is credited here anyway, with a link to its original page; the Freesound recording requires this credit, and has it, although it is not played.

Each recording has been edited for the game: trimmed, cut into single sounds or made into seamless loops, and set to a steady loudness. The game works them into its own sound design, so these files are part of the game and are not offered as a sound collection of their own. Please do not take them out of the game to redistribute them on their own; get them from Pixabay instead.

Every other sound in the game, including all beacons, the event item sound sets (pumpkin, bell, gem, star, snowflake and heart), the false bells of Call of Fate, the light's shimmering bell, the cathedral bell and the event ambiences cathedral and night, warnings, enemies, the driving engine and cars, and the menus, is generated by the game itself and has no outside source.
