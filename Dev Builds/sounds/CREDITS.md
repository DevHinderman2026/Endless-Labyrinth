# Sound Credits

Every recorded sound in Endless Labyrinth comes from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) and is used under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). The license allows free use, including in games, and allows editing. It does not require credit, but every recording is credited here anyway, with a link to its original page.

Each recording has been edited for the game: trimmed, cut into single sounds or made into seamless loops, and set to a steady loudness. The game works them into its own sound design, so these files are part of the game and are not offered as a sound collection of their own. Please do not take them out of the game to redistribute them on their own; get them from Pixabay instead.

Every other sound in the game, including all beacons, warnings, enemies, the driving engine and cars, and the menus, is generated by the game itself and has no outside source.
